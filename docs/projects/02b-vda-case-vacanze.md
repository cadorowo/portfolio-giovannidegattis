# VDA Case Vacanze — Vacation Rental Management & Hospitality Network

## Overview
- **Category / Tags**: Hospitality, Brand Identity, Web Design, Corporate Identity
- **Role**: Brand Identity Designer & Digital Strategist
- **Context**: Private Client (Property management agency & vacation rental network across Valle d'Aosta)
- **Year**: 2025
- **Deliverables**: Corporate Visual Identity System, Logo Mark & Symbol, Typography & Graphic Patterns, Multi-property Digital Platform Mockup
- **Available Assets**: High-Res Identity Presentation, Vector Logo Symbol, Website Mockups, Typography Pattern Spreads

## Key Narrative & Focus Areas
1. **Scalable Agency Brand Architecture**:
   - Distinct, professional brand identity built for an umbrella agency managing multiple rental properties.
   - Balances alpine warmth with corporate credibility for property owners and travelers.
2. **Symbol & Geometric Mark**:
   - Modern mark blending mountain peaks with geometric architectural silhouettes.
   - Flexible application across physical signage, keys, uniforms, and digital platforms.
3. **Multi-Property Digital Platform**:
   - Clean UI designed for multi-property browsing, location-based filtering, and direct inquiry management.
