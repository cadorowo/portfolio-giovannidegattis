# Netway — On-The-Road European Learning Experience & Brand Identity

## Overview
- **Category / Tags**: Brand Identity, Experiential Design, Social Media, Academic / PoliTO
- **Badge / Origin**: Politecnico di Torino (Academic Project)
- **Role**: Brand Identity Designer & Visual Strategist
- **Context**: Politecnico di Torino Design Studio
- **Year**: 2025
- **Deliverables**: Comprehensive Brand Identity, Experiential Concept, Instagram Visual Campaigns & Collateral, Key Art
- **Available Assets**: Brand Deliverables & Multi-Device Collateral Cover Mockup (`thumb.webp`, `cover.jpg`), Figma Source Files, Instagram Post System, Conceptual & Spatial Visual Assets

## Key Narrative & Focus Areas
1. **Nomadic & Experiential Learning Concept**:
   - Designing the complete identity for a pan-European educational journey on wheels (learning bus experience).
   - Translating movement, intercultural exchange, and hands-on education into dynamic graphic elements.
2. **Brand Identity & Visual Voice**:
   - Distinctive typography, vibrant palette, and flexible graphic patterns adaptable to vehicle wraps, digital screens, and merchandise.
3. **Social & Community Rollout**:
   - Instagram campaign architecture designed to document the journey, engage international students, and communicate route milestones.
