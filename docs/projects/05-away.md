## Key Narrative & Focus Areas
1. **Phygital Service Design & Court Immersion**:
   - Designing an integrated physical-to-digital journey bridging the physical Palavillage courts with immersive digital displays, real-time match tracking, and responsive lighting/audio cues.
2. **User Research & Service Blueprints**:
   - In-depth player and operator user research, customer journey maps, and service choreography from booking to post-match replay.
3. **Mobile Product & Design System**:
   - Dark, dynamic sports-tech UI crafted for quick glances and one-handed interactions before, during, and after play.
   - Scalable token-based design system with robust components and rich motion feedback.
4. **Tangible Artifacts (3D Printed Kit)**:
   - Physical prototyping of custom 3D printed components and on-court tokens demonstrating true end-to-end product design thinking.
