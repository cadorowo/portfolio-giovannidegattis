# Vivaldi Calendario — Experimental Wooden Calendar & Tangible Time Object

## Overview
- **Category / Tags**: Tangible Product Design, Physical Prototyping, Experimental Typography, Academic / PoliTO
- **Badge / Origin**: Politecnico di Torino (Academic Project)
- **Role**: Product Designer & Material Prototyper
- **Context**: Politecnico di Torino Product & Graphic Design Workshop
- **Year**: 2023
- **Deliverables**: Physical Modular Wooden Calendar, Precision-crafted Wooden Plates / Panels, Laser Cut / Wood Engraving System, Packaging & Presentation
- **Available Assets**: High-Resolution Physical Product Photography, Material & Joinery Close-ups

## Key Narrative & Focus Areas
1. **Materiality & Physical Tactility**:
   - Reinterpreting time through natural wood grain, precision joinery, and tactile day-to-day physical interactions.
2. **Harmonic Rhythm & Conceptual Foundation**:
   - Translating Vivaldi's musical cadence and cyclical seasons into tangible physical panels and spatial progression.
3. **Prototyping & Fabrication Craft**:
   - From digital CAD/vector blueprints to physical woodworking, laser engraving, and surface finishing.
